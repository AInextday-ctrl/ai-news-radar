Editorial Policy & Fact-Checking

Our comprehensive framework governing empirical verification, AI usage disclosure, code reproducibility, and corrections.

1. Verification & Multi-Source Cross-Checking

At AI News Radar, we believe that fast information is worthless if it is inaccurate. Every intelligence item, breaking news alert, and technical article undergoes a strict verification funnel prior to public indexation:

  • Primary Source Requirement: We do not report on secondary blog summaries. Every major model release, API update, or benchmark claim must be verified against official engineering blog posts, whitepapers, or repository commits.
  • Multi-Source Triangulation: For industry developments or unverified rumors, we require independent confirmation from at least two credible sources before publishing.
  • Code Re-Execution: Technical tutorials must be executed in clean, isolated Docker environments to confirm that all CLI commands, libraries, and dependencies function without runtime deprecation errors.

2. Responsible AI Usage Disclosure (Google Compliance)

In full accordance with Google Search Essentials and AdSense Quality Guidelines regarding AI-generated/assisted content:

  • Human Authorship & Accountability: All flagship architecture teardowns and benchmark reports are written, reviewed, and finalized by credentialed human engineers (Alex Lin, Marcus Zhang, Dr. Elena Wang).
  • AI Assistance Role: Where Large Language Models are employed—such as translating multi-language tweets or generating structured JSON feeds—the output is rigorously edited by our editorial staff to eradicate hallucinations, tone exaggeration, or technical misinterpretations.
  • Zero Synthetic Slop: We enforce an absolute ban on unreviewed, bulk-generated AI content. Our metric of success is engineering utility, not raw page count.

3. Commercial Independence & Conflicts of Interest

Our readers trust us because our reviews cannot be purchased:

  • No Sponsored Benchmark Placement: AI laboratories and cloud providers cannot pay to alter their ranking on our benchmarks or in our comparative guides.
  • Transparent Testing Hardware: We disclose exact CPU, GPU, memory, and OS specifications for every test performed in our lab.
  • Clear Labeling of Affiliate Links: If outbound links to developer tools or cloud services contain referral identifiers, they are routed through nofollow/ugc redirects and clearly disclosed. Editorial opinions are never influenced by affiliate programs.

4. Corrections, Errata & Retractions Policy

We are committed to swift transparency whenever errors occur:

  • Minor Corrections: Typographical or minor numerical errors that do not affect technical conclusions are amended promptly, with an update note appended at the foot of the article.
  • Substantive Errata: If a code sample, performance benchmark, or factual claim is found to be inaccurate, we publish a prominent Correction Notice at the top of the article detailing what was corrected, the date of amendment, and the technical rationale.
  • Reporting Inaccuracies: If you identify a discrepancy or failing code sample, please notify our editorial desk at editorial@ainewsradar.xyz. We pledge an initial response within 24 business hours.
