of AI being used for the sexual exploitation of children have jumped more than 8,400 percent in two years. According to a report by Edinburgh's Childlight Institute, the number of reported AI-generated abuse images climbed from about 4,700 in 2023 to over 400,000 in 2025, The Guardian reports. The material includes AI-manipulated images (deepfakes) from so-called "nudify apps" that digitally strip people of their clothing. Abusers can use the faked images to blackmail children into handing over money or more photos.

The data comes from reports that tech companies filed with the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). The UK's Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) reviewed 6,310 criminal, photorealistic AI-generated abuse images in the first half of 2026 alone. That's 40 percent more than in all of 2025.

Childlight data director Debi Fry acknowledges that better detection systems partly explain the spike. She still calls for swift action from lawmakers and tech companies.
