📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Waymo has raised $5 billion in debt from private credit lenders without parent guarantees from Alphabet, outlining a hyper-aggressive fleet expansion targeting 8,000 vehicles by year-end (up from 4,000) and 20,000 by 2027. Operating across 15 cities and expanding to international hubs like Toronto, the company is sourcing custom electric vehicles from Zeekr to drive down unit costs while planning fleet lease securitizations to cement a self-sustaining asset-heavy mobility business.
