📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

CNBC Full Report

Altimeter Capital is aiming to invest $5 billion in the Anthropic IPO, Brad Gerstner told CNBC's "Halftime Report" on Monday.

"I think this is a cornerstone compounder for a long time," Gerstner said, noting that his investment would be five times the amount he's ever put into an IPO. "It's where the trade is going."

Anthropic is expected to go on Nasdaq on Thursday in a milestone event for the artificial intelligence industry. It would be the first major AI lab to go public, beating OpenAI, which has said it's targeting an IPO next year.

Gerstner is investing alongside the leading chipmakers and cloud providers, many of which count on Anthropic as a customer.

Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company are collectively investing $26 billion at the IPO price, which is currently expected at $70 to $78 a share.

Anthropic is planning to offer 825 million shares at a valuation of about $1.4 trillion.

Gerstner is friends with former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, and sent a letter to the board of directors last week pushing them to take their time and run a full investigation on Huang's tenure before deciding who should run the company.

Gerstner said he has confidence in CFO and interim CEO Colette Kress, and noted that Huang had not discussed a comeback as CEO with him.

While Anthropic is hitting the market at a particularly tough moment — coming after a downturn in semiconductors, a massive anti-AI swing in Washington, D.C., and the Huang saga — Gerstner said he's still optimistic.

"They have done such an excellent job presenting this company, they're in such an excellent position on the financials," Gerstner said, "that folks like me are making this a major order."
