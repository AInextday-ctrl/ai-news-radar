Altimeter's Brad Gerstner aims to invest $5 billion in Anthropic IPO

✍️ By Kif Leswing

Brad Gerstner, founder of Altimeter Capital, announced plans to invest $5 billion in the Anthropic IPO, marking his largest-ever IPO commitment. The offering has secured a collective $26 billion investment from tech giants including AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC, with the company targeting a $1.4 trillion valuation ahead of its Thursday Nasdaq listing.

⚡ Executive Summary & Key Facts

  • [Core Event] Brad Gerstner (Founder of Altimeter Capital), Anthropic (Nasdaq): Commitment of $5 billion for Anthropic's IPO at a $1.4 trillion valuation
  • [Root Cause & Mechanism] Strategic belief in Anthropic as a long-term compounding asset in the AI industry
  • [Key Metrics] Altimeter commits $5B as a long-term compounding cornerstone investment.
  • [Key Position & Outlook] Anthropic to list on Nasdaq this Thursday, marking the first major AI lab IPO.

📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

CNBC Full Report

Altimeter Capital is aiming to invest $5 billion in the Anthropic initial public offering, Brad Gerstner told CNBC's "Halftime Report"on Monday.

"I think this is a cornerstone compounder for a long time,"Gerstner said in an interview on the sidelines of Semafor's Silicon Valley and the World forum, noting that his investment would be five times the amount he's ever put into an IPO. "It's where the trade is going."

Anthropic is expected to go on Nasdaq on Thursday in a milestone event for the artificial intelligence industry. It would be the first major AI lab to go public, beating OpenAI, which has said it's targeting an IPO next year.

Gerstner is investing alongside the leading chipmakers and cloud providers, many of which count on Anthropic as a customer.

Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. are collectively investing $26 billion at the IPO price, which is currently expected at $70 to $78 a share.

Anthropic is planning to offer 825 million shares at a valuation of about $1.4 trillion.

Gerstner is friends with former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, and sent a letter to the board of directors last week pushing them to take their time and run a full investigation on Huang's tenure before deciding who should run the company.

Gerstner said he has confidence in CFO and interim CEO Colette Kress, and noted that Huang had not discussed a comeback as CEO with him.

While Anthropic is hitting the market at a particularly tough moment — coming after a downturn in semiconductors, a massive anti-AI swing in Washington and the Huang saga — Gerstner said he's still optimistic.

"They have done such an excellent job presenting this company, they're in such an excellent position on the financials,"Gerstner said, "that folks like me are making this a major order."

🤖 AI Radar Technical Deep-Dive

This unprecedented backing creates a deep strategic alignment between the AI compute supply chain and model developers, effectively securing control over next-gen AGI infrastructure. Pricing Anthropic at $1.4 trillion signals a shift where model provider valuations are pegged to hardware titans, reflecting a tactical consolidation to mitigate mounting geopolitical and regulatory pressures.
