Anthropic plans fund for workers threatened by AI, Musk counters with SpaceX shares for children

✍️ By THE DECODER

Anthropic unveiled plans to allocate 5% of its equity into a company-controlled public benefit fund designed to study direct payouts to workers displaced by AI automation. The corporate-owned structure drew immediate ridicule from Elon Musk, who blasted the move on X and demanded the shares be distributed directly to the public instead.

  • [Root Cause & Mechanism] Anthropic seeks moral high ground ahead of IPO, while critics attack the internal corporate control
  • [Key Metrics] Anthropic pledges 5% of equity to study direct cash aid for displaced labor.
  • [Key Position & Outlook] Musk lambasted the structure on X, telling them to give shares straight to the public.

📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

THE DECODER Full Report

Anthropic wants to put five percent of its shares into a new public benefit fund that will also look into direct payments to workers at risk of losing their jobs. The fund is a company-owned LLC, so Anthropic keeps control of it as a possible IPO approaches. By offering the option of direct payments, Anthropic goes further than its competitors. Elon Musk says on X the company should just give the shares directly to the public.
