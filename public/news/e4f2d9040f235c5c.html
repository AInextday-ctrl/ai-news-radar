📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

PCMag Full Report

The CyberLeek hacking gang claims to have released a key executable program that promises to help the modding community port Grand Theft Auto 6 to the PC. On Monday, the group updated its website, posting a link to the 57MB archive that contains the executable program. “The exe is out. Always free. An early Christmas gift from CL,” CyberLeek wrote in a post titled “Xmas came early.”

The archive supposedly completes the stolen Xbox build for GTA 6 that CyberLeek initially began circulating last week via a 209GB torrent containing files for Rockstar’s RAGE game engine and the Xbox console. CyberLeek now suggests that the file dump will be enough for the modding community to recompile the game to run on a PC, since the Xbox platform runs on an OS based on Microsoft Windows.

So far, Rockstar Games and its parent, Take-Two Interactive, haven’t responded to a request for comment. But developers are rumored to be preparing an official PC release for GTA 6 as soon as next year’s first quarter. Still, a pirated PC port could go viral and sap sales for the game, which is currently exclusive to the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. The dumped files also suggest CyberLeek was able to infiltrate and steal an Xbox copy of GTA 6 well before the game officially launched. Although Take-Two subpoenaed the major platforms, including Microsoft, for records to try to uncover the leaker, CyberLeek remains at large.
