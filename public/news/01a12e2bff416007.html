Pinterest reports Q3 revenue up 12% YoY to $1.17B, vs. $1.21B est., global MAUs up 10% to 661M, and forecasts Q4 revenue below est.; PINS drops 8%+ after hours (Liz Moyer/Barron's Online)

✍️ By Barron's Online

Pinterest reported Q3 revenue of $1.17 billion, up 12% year-over-year but missing analyst estimates of $1.21 billion, while global MAUs expanded 10% to 661 million. A disappointing Q4 revenue forecast fueled worries over its monetization efficiency, sending the stock tumbling more than 8% in after-hours trading.

⚡ Executive Summary & Key Facts

  • [Core Event] Pinterest: Q3 revenue of $1.17B missed expectations and Q4 guidance was weak, causing shares to sink over 8%
  • [Root Cause & Mechanism] Despite 10% user growth, intense digital ad competition hampered monetization conversion
  • [Key Metrics] Q3 revenue rose 12% to $1.17B, falling short of Wall Street's $1.21B estimate.
  • [Key Position & Outlook] Subpar Q4 revenue guidance triggered an 8%+ after-hours selloff.

🤖 AI Radar Technical Deep-Dive

Users love pinning aesthetic boards, but they clearly aren't buying the ads. In a cutthroat ad market dominated by mega-caps, having 660 million passive scrollers won't stop Wall Street from pulling the plug.
