Cloudflare debuts Clairic, a System One-class extraction model it says processes unstructured data faster, more cheaply, and more accurately than normal LLMs (Cloudflare)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Cloudflare unveiled Clairic, a specialized 'System One' extraction model hosted on Workers AI designed to extract, compute, and forecast values from unstructured inputs. The company claims it delivers superior accuracy, lower latency, and drastically lower costs compared to traditional LLMs, with plans to open-source model weights soon.
