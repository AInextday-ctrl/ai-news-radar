Qwen4-Max scores 43 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, beating Qwen3.8 Max at ~67% lower cost per task but trailing Kimi K3.1 Pro at ~11% lower cost (Artificial Analysis)

✍️ By Artificial Analysis

Alibaba's Qwen4-Max scored 43 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, surpassing the median of 41. It supports a 984k context window with pricing set at $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens, achieving significant cost-efficiency gains over its predecessor, Qwen3.8 Max.

⚡ Executive Summary & Key Facts

  • [Core Event] Alibaba Qwen Team: Released Qwen4-Max with a score of 43 and reduced pricing
  • [Root Cause & Mechanism] To capture commercial inference market share by optimizing architecture for superior intelligence-to-cost ratios
  • [Key Metrics] Cost per task reduced by approximately 67% compared to the Qwen3.8 Max.
  • [Key Position & Outlook] Model features a 984k context window and exhibits higher-than-average token verbosity during benchmark inference.

Artificial Analysis Full Report

Qwen4 Max is above average in intelligence and reasonably priced when comparing to other models of similar price. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 984k tokens context window.

Qwen4 Max scores 43 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it above average among comparable models (median: 41). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 127M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 78.6M.

Pricing for Qwen4 Max is $1.50 per 1M input tokens (moderately priced, median: $2.00) and $4.50 per 1M output tokens (moderately priced, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $3.27 per task to evaluate Qwen4 Max on the Intelligence Index.
