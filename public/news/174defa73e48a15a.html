Ant-backed Mynt, which runs mobile wallet app GCash, rose as much as 2.4% in Manila after raising $841M in the Philippines' largest-ever IPO, valuing it at ~$7B (Kimberley Kao/Wall Street Journal)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Ant Group-backed fintech firm Mynt, operator of the dominant Philippine mobile wallet GCash, saw its shares rise as much as 2.4% during its debut on the Manila stock exchange. The company raised over $841 million in the country's largest-ever IPO, pushing its valuation to roughly $7 billion on the back of widespread unbanked population adoption.
