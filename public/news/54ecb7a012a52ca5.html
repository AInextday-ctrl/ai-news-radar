Google updates Gemini Privacy Hub to extend its retention period for temporary chats and chats with history turned off from 72 hours to around two months (Jagmeet Singh/TechCrunch)

✍️ By TechCrunch

Google updated its Gemini Apps Privacy Hub to extend the retention period for temporary chats from 72 hours to approximately two months. The company stated this change aims to improve system safety and incident response, explicitly noting that these conversations remain excluded from AI model training. Additionally, Google decoupled chat history retention from model training permissions, allowing users to maintain logs without contributing to model development.

🤖 AI Radar Technical Deep-Dive

The retention extension reflects Google's balancing act between data compliance and system robustness, leveraging the decoupling of storage from model training to secure a wider window for security auditing without violating user trust. This strategic shift signals an industry-wide move toward granular data control, aimed at navigating tightening global data protection regulations.

📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

TechCrunch Full Report

Google is extending how long it keeps temporary Gemini chats and conversations made with chat history turned off to around two months, saying the change aims to improve safety and security as AI systems become more capable.

The change, which will roll out globally in the Gemini app over the coming weeks, marks a significant increase from the previous 72-hour retention period mentioned in Google’s Gemini Apps Privacy Hub, according to an archived version from November 12.

OpenAI, by comparison, says it may keep temporary chats in ChatGPT for up to 30 days, whereas Anthropic retains incognito chats in Claude for 30 days by default. However, companies may keep some data longer for legal or safety reasons, or to investigate policy violations.

Google did not answer questions about what prompted the change and whether it would affect how conversations are accessed or reviewed, instead pointing to a blog post that says chats are kept longer to respond to users, process feedback, and protect its services and users. Its updated Gemini Apps Privacy Hub also cites potential system failures as a reason for retaining chats and says temporary conversations are not used to train AI models.

The longer retention period comes as Google introduces separate settings for Gemini’s chat history and AI training. Until now, both were tied to a single “Keep Activity” setting. Users will now be able to keep their conversations in their chat history without using them to train Google’s generative AI models.

Google says both new settings will be turned off by default for users who previously disabled Keep Activity. For those who had Keep Activity turned on, their chat history will remain enabled. They can, though, decide separately whether Google can use their conversations to train AI models.
