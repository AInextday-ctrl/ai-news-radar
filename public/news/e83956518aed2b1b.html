Walmart is inviting AI agents to shop for you

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Walmart announced plans to permit customers to link personal AI agents directly to its e-commerce platform starting next month, enabling automated shopping within strict spending limits and manual review gates. The retailer laid out clear boundary guidelines to prevent unauthorized payment modifications and out-of-scope actions. The welcoming approach directly counters Amazon's hostile strategy of aggressively banning third-party shopping agents.
