Reliable Robotics, which develops an autonomous flight system, raised a $115M extension for its $160M Series D, pushing its valuation to $1B+ (Kirsten Korosec/TechCrunch)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Reliable Robotics, a developer of autonomous flight systems for retrofitting existing aircraft, has secured a $115 million extension to its Series D round, elevating its valuation past the $1 billion unicorn threshold. The round comprises $90 million in equity led by Nimble Partners alongside $25 million in venture debt. The fresh capital will extend the company's operational runway to achieve critical FAA airworthiness certification for commercial unmanned operations.
