Anthropic raises $93.5 billion total as underwriters scoop up ‘greenshoe’ overallotment

✍️ By Ashley Capoot

AI firm Anthropic completed its historic IPO on Nasdaq, raising a total of $93.5 billion after underwriters exercised the greenshoe option for an additional $12.2 billion. Simultaneously, the company closed a $26 billion private placement involving major tech entities like AMD, Google, Microsoft, and Nvidia, bringing total gross proceeds to $119.5 billion.

⚡ Executive Summary & Key Facts

  • [Core Event] Anthropic (CEO Dario Amodei, Co-founder Daniela Amodei) (Nasdaq Stock Market): Completed IPO of 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83/share plus greenshoe, and a concurrent private placement
  • [Root Cause & Mechanism] To secure massive capital for multi-trillion dollar AI infrastructure deployment
  • [Key Metrics] IPO raised $93.5 billion total, covering 1.127 billion Class A shares plus overallotment.
  • [Key Position & Outlook] Stock volatility post-IPO reflects market caution amid broader tech sell-offs and valuation concerns.

📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

CNBC Full Report

Anthropic underwriters have officially exercised their overallotment of shares in the historic initial public offering, bringing the total raised to $93.5 billion, according to a release on Friday.

The artificial intelligence company raised $81.3 billion on Wednesday and net an additional $12.2 billion through the overallotment, surpassing SpaceX, which held a record IPO in June.

Anthropic said it sold an aggregate of 1,127,000,000 shares of its Class A common stock at $83 per share in the offering. It sold 980 million shares on Wednesday and its underwriters had the option to purchase up to an additional 147 million shares by Friday as part of the overallotment, which is commonly referred to as the "greenshoe."

Shares of the Claude maker opened trading at $111.50 on the Nasdaq Thursday, but slid throughout the day and closed at $97.17. The stock sank 12% in pre-market trading on Friday during a broad tech sell-off.

In addition to the sale of its Class A common stock, Anthropic closed a concurrent sale of its Class N common stock after a group of tech companies, including Advanced Micro Devices, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia and others, agreed to participate in a $26 billion private placement at the IPO price. Anthropic said Friday that aggregate gross proceeds from its IPO, including the private placement, totaled $119.5 billion, before deducting offering expenses.

Anthropic dodged an unforgiving series of obstacles on its road to the Nasdaq on Thursday, as investors brooded over Jensen Huang's shocking ouster at Nvidia, voters' stinging rebuke of the tech during the midterms and whether rising interest rates would stymie industry plans for a multi-trillion-dollar AI infrastructure buildout.

CEO Dario Amodei co-founded the company in 2021 after he and a handful of other researchers, including his sister Daniela, quit their jobs at OpenAI, now Anthropic's chief rival. Anthropic is best known for developing a family of AI models called Claude, which have captivated the corporate world and fueled the company's historic rise.

The siblings celebrated the IPO as a "milestone"in an interview with CNBC on Thursday, but said there's more work ahead for Anthropic.

"For us, it's like one stop on the journey,"Daniela Amodei said. "We think about this as, 'Wow, this is an incredible moment for Anthropic for our mission, but we're going to wake up tomorrow and we're going to be the same company.'"

🤖 AI Radar Technical Deep-Dive

Anthropic's financing effectively cements a strategic coalition with AMD, Nvidia, and hyperscalers, signaling that the capital barrier for AI infrastructure has escalated to the $100 billion scale. Despite market volatility and interest rate headwinds, the move anchors key industry players as long-term shareholders, guaranteeing supply chain stability for its Claude model ecosystem.
