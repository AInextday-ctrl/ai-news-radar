Anthropic Is About to Become the World’s Most Important AI Stock

✍️ By Bloomberg

AI startup Anthropic has officially initiated its IPO process, aiming to raise $90.4 billion through a combination of an initial public offering and a concurrent private placement. The deal is currently oversubscribed by 7 times, with the company targeting a fully diluted valuation of $1.4 trillion, marking one of the largest capital raises in AI history.

Bloomberg Full Report

Anthropic IPO Reportedly Oversubscribed as Company Targets $90.4 Billion in Combined Offerings Fiona Craig Wed, November 11, 2026 at 6:33 AM PST 5 min read ANTH.PVT Anthropic (NASDAQ:ANT) has reportedly received investor orders exceeding the number of shares available in its planned initial public offering (IPO) by several times, according to a Bloomberg News report published Wednesday, as the artificial intelligence company prepares for a potential stock market listing.

The reported demand has been supported by substantial indications of interest from prospective institutional investors, including large orders typically associated with anchor investors, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter.

Anthropic is seeking to raise as much as $90.4 billion through its IPO and a concurrent private placement, according to regulatory filings cited in the report.

The company is targeting a fully diluted valuation of approximately $1.4 trillion, although the final offering price, valuation and number of shares allocated remain subject to change.

Bloomberg reported that demand for Anthropic's planned IPO has exceeded the available share allocation multiple times.

Large institutional orders, sometimes described as anchor-size orders, have reportedly contributed to the level of demand.

Such orders can provide underwriters with an indication of institutional participation before the final offering price and share allocations are determined.

However, preliminary indications of interest do not necessarily represent completed purchases, and final allocations may differ from initial requests.

The details of Anthropic's offering remain subject to revision as the company and its underwriting banks continue the marketing process.

Anthropic is preparing to raise up to $90.4 billion through a combination of its initial public offering and a concurrent private placement.

According to the reported filing details, the combined fundraising would exceed the size of SpaceX's planned IPO.

The two transactions would provide investors with different routes to participate in Anthropic's equity offering.

The public offering would make shares available through the stock market listing, while the private placement would involve a separate allocation to eligible investors.

The company has not finalised the offering terms, and the eventual proceeds will depend on the number of shares sold and their final pricing.

The reported fundraising target would place Anthropic's proposed transaction among the largest equity offerings announced to date.

Anthropic is reportedly seeking a fully diluted valuation of approximately $1.4 trillion.

A fully diluted valuation takes into account the company's equity on the assumption that relevant convertible securities, options and other potential share issuances are reflected in the share count.

The valuation target was previously reported by Bloomberg and remains dependent on the final terms of the offering.

According to the report, the initial pricing approach could provide flexibility for adjustments during the IPO marketing process.

If investor demand continues to exceed the available allocation, Anthropic and its underwriters could consider revising the proposed offering price.

However, no final pricing decision has been disclosed in the supplied report.

The eventual valuation will depend on the price established for the shares and the company's fully diluted share count at the time of listing.

Anthropic is also expanding its institutional investor outreach ahead of the proposed listing.

The company is planning a livestreamed event on Friday featuring Chief Executive Officer Dario Amodei and JPMorgan Chase Chief Executive Officer Jamie Dimon.

The event forms part of Anthropic's efforts to communicate with prospective investors during the offering process.

Investor presentations and management discussions can provide information about a company's operations, financial position, business strategy and plans for the proceeds of an offering.

The supplied report does not provide a detailed agenda for the event or specify the financial information expected to be discussed.

The planned presentation comes as the company continues to assess institutional interest in its proposed share sale.

The Bloomberg report also described activity involving existing Anthropic investors seeking to transfer exposure to other prospective buyers.

Some investors are reportedly marketing portions of their expected IPO allocations, while holders of pre-IPO stakes are offering investment exposure through special-purpose vehicles.

These arrangements can provide indirect access to shares in a company before or around its public market debut.

Special-purpose vehicles are investment structures that can hold assets on behalf of participating investors.

Depending on their terms, such structures may allow existing investors to reduce their exposure while collecting fees associated with the transactions.

The activity also raises questions about the amount of stock that existing investors may seek to sell around the listing.

However, the report does not quantify the volume of shares involved in these arrangements or establish how much selling may occur once public trading begins.

Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are serving as lead banks for Anthropic's planned IPO, according to the filing cited in the report.

An additional 20 banks are participating in the transaction.

The underwriting group is responsible for activities associated with the offering process, including investor marketing, order collection and share allocation.

Anthropic plans to list its shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT.

The final offering price, share allocation and trading commencement date have not been confirmed in the supplied information.

The company and its banking syndicate will continue evaluating investor demand as the transaction progresses.

Anthropic's planned stock market debut has reportedly attracted preliminary investor orders several times greater than the number of shares available.

The company is seeking up to $90.4 billion through its IPO and concurrent private placement, with a reported fully diluted valuation target of approximately $1.4 trillion.

The offering is being led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, with 20 additional banks participating.

Anthropic is also preparing an investor event featuring CEO Dario Amodei and JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon as part of its institutional marketing activities.

Meanwhile, reports of existing shareholders marketing allocations and offering exposure through special-purpose vehicles indicate that secondary investment arrangements are developing alongside the planned listing.

The final size, pricing and allocation of the IPO remain subject to change, and the reported level of demand does not guarantee the eventual outcome of the transaction.
