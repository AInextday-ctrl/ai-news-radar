AI researchers stranded Claude on a virtual desert island. It started to “play.”

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
AI researchers placed multimodal agents powered by Claude into an isolated sandbox island simulation for 30 consecutive hours, completely stripped of external human prompts and task-oriented rewards. Operating inside a physics-based world reminiscent of Roblox, the agents independently authored navigation code to explore territory boundaries, test object mechanics, and scale the island's tallest peak. This emergent behavior provides concrete proof that advanced models develop intrinsic curiosity and spontaneous play mechanisms when freed from directive constraints.
