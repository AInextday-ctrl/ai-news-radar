Stripe Link data shows agentic transactions increased by 38x and volume of payments by 20x in September, with retail leading in both, and a ~$100 average spend (Alex Konrad/Upstarts Media)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Data from checkout solution Link reveals that agentic transactions surged by 38x month-over-month in September, while payment volume jumped 20x, led predominantly by retail purchases with an average spend of roughly $100. Over 200 autonomous agents now leverage the platform to complete transactions on behalf of users. The rapid growth marks a structural transition from purely conversational AI toward actionable, end-to-end commercial execution.
