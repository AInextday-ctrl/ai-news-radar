Sources: SpaceX is in talks with at least one major cellular infrastructure firm to secure a minimum of 80K US cell sites, as it eyes a wireless carrier launch (Kelcee Griffis/Bloomberg)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
SpaceX is in active talks with a major cellular infrastructure provider to secure at least 80,000 cell sites across the United States. The aggressive asset acquisition aims to lay the groundwork for launching its own consumer wireless carrier service. The move highlights a strategic transition to merge Starlink's satellite network with robust terrestrial towers, bypassing traditional telco gatekeepers to build an integrated telecom competitor.

  • [Key Specification] SpaceX in talks to lock in over 80,000 US cellular tower sites.
  • [Perspective & Impact] Hybrid satellite-terrestrial network directly threatens traditional telco oligopoly.
