📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

OpenAI Full Report

Introducing computer-use agents, improved clinical answers, and new healthcare plugins that work across healthcare software to get real work done.

Healthcare teams need to piece together information from the EHR, hospital policies, scheduling systems, and payer portals to deliver care and manage operations. With ChatGPT for Healthcare, we’re bringing the tools teams already use and the medical evidence they need into one platform to help them spend less time navigating systems and more time caring for patients.

Today, we’re sharing advances in computer-use agents, which lets AI agents carry out multi-step work across existing healthcare software. We’re also improving trusted clinical search, which finds, evaluates, and cites medical evidence, and introducing more than 20 new plugins to the healthcare plugin ecosystem.

Together, these capabilities help healthcare teams find the information they need, make decisions grounded in medical evidence, and carry out next steps across the systems they already use.

Carry work forward across healthcare systems with computer-use agents

Organizations often need to move information between disconnected systems to prepare documentation, coordinate care, and complete administrative tasks. Computer-use agents can help with these steps by navigating the software that teams already use: reading screens, clicking, typing, and moving between applications.

Today, we’re sharing advances in computer-use agents for healthcare, including more capable, safe, and affordable models for clinical and administrative workflows, and access controls and audit logs for secure deployments. We’re also sharing our work with healthcare organizations that are deploying agents, including Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare, and CVS Health, to process referrals, move documents between systems, and bring together the information care teams need. In the demo below, a clinician uses Voice in the ChatGPT app to ask for help following up on an MRI request. The agent reviews the chart and drafts an order and patient reply for review.

Healthcare organizations can start with a workflow that spans existing applications, set which applications agents can access, and establish clear controls for which steps require human review. To support HIPAA-compliant workflows involving protected health information, organizations must use eligible services covered by their BAA with OpenAI, apply required security and retention settings, and extend protections to connected systems. Content shared with ChatGPT for Healthcare is not used to train models.

Read more about our computer use research, customer stories, and demonstrations in Advancing computer-use agents for healthcare teams.

Stronger clinical answers, everywhere teams work

We’re improving trusted clinical search in ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Clinicians, including how ChatGPT finds, evaluates, and cites medical evidence. These updates help clinicians connect relevant evidence to clinical questions and translate it into practical next steps, with citations they can inspect.

To measure the impact of these updates, in a blind evaluation, physicians reviewed responses to real-world clinical questions across multiple specialties, including multi-turn conversations. In over 1300 reviews, physicians rated the updated experience more highly across all eight dimensions evaluated, including factual correctness, safety, actionability, and source authoritativeness.

Bring healthcare tools into one conversation

We’re expanding our healthcare plugin ecosystem with more than 20 new healthcare plugins from leading companies across clinical care, care coordination, and business operations. All of these are available today in ChatGPT for Healthcare, including through ChartGPT, which brings ChatGPT into the EHR workflow.

These plugins let teams bring more of their work into the same conversation: reviewing patient records and hospital protocols, preparing prescriptions for clinician review, coordinating patient outreach, checking insurance eligibility, and analyzing healthcare costs and operational performance.

See how healthcare teams can use these plugins together in ChatGPT.

Care coordinator | Resolve barriers before the visit

Use Luma Health to find tomorrow’s appointments that still need confirmation, then check those patients’ insurance eligibility with TriZetto. Build a call list showing what I need to confirm or resolve with each patient before their visit.

Primary care physician | Follow up after a hospital stay

My patient was hospitalized elsewhere for heart failure - find the discharge summary using Metriport. Which medications changed? What follow-up monitoring does UpToDate recommend for those changes? Link me to both sources.

Inpatient nurse | Navigate complex care

I’m admitting a patient who uses their own insulin pump, and the admitting team has requested endocrinology input. Pull up our hospital’s inpatient pump policy in AIVA, including required nursing checks and documentation, and use Amion to find the covering endocrinologist and how to reach them.

Care navigator | Compare two sites for an MRI

My patient can get a knee MRI without contrast at either of these two hospitals. Use IMO Health to identify the matching procedure code, check their insurance eligibility and benefits in Stedi, then show me their plan’s negotiated rate for that code at each hospital using Turquoise Health. Also identify any additional information needed to estimate what the patient would owe.

Referral manager | Review referrals and scheduling follow-ups

For our weekly referral review, use Assort Health to summarize referral volume, conversion trends, and our main referring practices. Use Ferry Health to show appointment tasks by status and link to those awaiting follow-up. Put the referral trends and outstanding scheduling work into a brief my team can use to prioritize patient follow-up.

The new plugins join ChatGPT’s broader ecosystem of plugins supporting work across industries, from healthcare data and research to documents, presentations, and software development.

With our plugin partners, we’re making it easier for care teams to bring clinical evidence, patient information, and their everyday tools into ChatGPT. We’re continuing to invest in this ecosystem and inviting more healthcare companies to build with us.

Availability and next steps

ChatGPT for Healthcare supports clinical, research, and administrative teams in a governed workspace. ChatGPT for Clinicians is free for eligible, verified clinicians in the U.S. to review medical evidence and prepare clinical documentation. Both products offer customers a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with OpenAI to support HIPAA-compliant workflows.

ChatGPT for Healthcare customers: Computer-use agents in ChatGPT Work and Codex and improved trusted clinical search are available today, subject to workspace settings. Ask your workspace administrator to enable new healthcare plugins.

Developers exploring computer use: Computer-use agent capability is available in the OpenAI API, ChatGPT Work, and Codex. Contact our sales team to discuss using these capabilities in your clinical and administrative workflows.
