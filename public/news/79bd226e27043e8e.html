Ubisoft Logs Strong Bookings as Players Welcome Assassin’s Creed Remake

✍️ By Wall Street Journal

Ubisoft Singapore's 'Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced' achieved 3.5 million sales within two weeks, accompanied by recent New Game+ and difficulty updates. Jean Guesdon, Head of AC Content, confirmed in the Fall 2026 Update that no further remakes are in development, with resources redirected to 'Codename Invictus' and the mainline 'Codename Hexe'.

  • [Core Event] Jean Guesdon, Head of AC Content at Ubisoft; Ubisoft Singapore (Fall 2026 Update report): Confirmed sales success of 'Black Flag Resynced', halted further remake projects, and provided updates on 'Codename Invictus' and 'Codename Hexe
  • [Root Cause & Mechanism] Reallocating R&D resources to prioritize multiplayer expansion and future mainline sequels
  • [Key Fact Detail] Achieved 3.5 million units sold in two weeks, validating the commercial appeal of single-player remakes.

📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

Wall Street Journal Full Report

It's been almost four months now since Ubisoft released their first Assassin's Creed remake in the form of Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced, a single-player Edward Kenway-focused remake of 2013's Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag.

Black Flag Resynced wound up becoming one of Ubisoft's most successful games in recent years, having sold over 3.5 million copies by its second week alone, and to help keep fans interested in the remake, Ubisoft Singapore recently added New Game+ as well as Nightmare difficulty to the pirate game.

A day after that update launched, AC franchise Head of Content Jean Guesdon released a Fall 2026 Update article detailing the future for the series, with one of the more disappointing revelations being that Ubisoft currently has no other AC remake in development, suggesting that a remake of Assassin's Creed 1 won't be launching alongside its 20th anniversary next year.

Guesdon started the Fall 2026 Update article detailing the AC franchise's new leadership and plans to better connect with the fan community by hosting several closed-door workshops throughout the remainder of the year with "dedicated community members and content creators."

Guesdon additionally announced that the New Game+ update won't be the last players see of new content for Black Flag Resynced as Ubisoft Singapore will be releasing several quality-of-life updates to the game over the next few months.

However, there are no plans to add any more major content for Black Flag Resynced, much to the disappointment of fans hoping to see the Adewale-led Freedom Cry DLC be added to the remake or even feature a brand-new DLC storyline following Edward Kenway as an Assassin in England.

Guesdon went on to detail official new info about the upcoming PvP AC multiplayer game, Codename Invictus, with insiders reportedly responding very positive to the game's prototype parkour, cooperative play, ability to play as multiple famous AC characters, and map design.

Invictus is still a long way away from release, however, and won't likely launch until after the next mainline AC game, Assassin's Creed: Codename Hexe, debuts, with Guesdon reporting that Ubisoft is making steady progress on Hexe and will share more concrete details about the game in 2027.

With 2027 being the 20th anniversary year of the Assassin's Creed franchise, Ubisoft is prepping next year to be chock-full of reveals, updates, rewards, and community events for fans, but one thing fans shouldn't expect to see next year is a remake of Assassin's Creed 1.

In the Fall 2026 Update article, Guesdon acknowledged fan excitement about the potential of other AC remakes following Black Flag Resynced's success but reported that Ubisoft currently has no other AC remakes in active development.

Instead, all AC developer teams are hard at work on "the next chapter of Assassin's Creed titles" which includes games that haven't been publicly revealed yet, set in new settings and stories.

While the hint at more brand-new AC games is exciting, the news that no other AC remakes are in active development comes as a disappointment to many fans, as many expected Assassin's Creed 1 to be remade for the franchise's 20th anniversary in November 2027.

Despite starting the franchise, AC1 has been the most neglected game in the series as it hasn't been remastered or rereleased on any gaming platform beyond the seventh console generation.

With the game being nearly 20 years old, Assassin's Creed 1 is the AC game most in need of a proper remake as, while the game's story, gameplay, and visuals were great for 2007, not everything has aged perfectly well.

Most notably, AC1's gameplay feels sluggish and clunky compared to modern AC titles, and it would be nice to see developers like Ubisoft Bordeaux tackle a remake of AC1 given that Assassin's Creed Mirage was meant to harken back AC1's more stealth-focused style of gameplay.

It's unlikely that Ubisoft would be able to start and finish development of an AC1 remake in 2027, but that shouldn't stop them from starting to work on an AC1 remake that could potentially debut in 2028 or 2029.

A remake of AC1 could not only make it easier for younger AC fans to witness Altair and Desmond Miles' stories, but help expand both characters' history with the remake potentially including story elements from Assassin's Creed: Bloodlines, Assassin's Creed: Revelations, and even events only hinted at in games and books such as the backstory of Altair's parents.

Remaking AC1 would also be a good starting point toward the goal of eventually remaking the Ezio trilogy, as those games feature some of the most popular stories of the AC franchise and Ubisoft is sure to want to profit from fans longing to relive the life of Ezio Auditore in a new way.
