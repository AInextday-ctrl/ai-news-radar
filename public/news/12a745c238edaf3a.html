Meta reports Q3 revenue up 26% YoY to $64.6B, operating income down 37% to $12.99B on a $9.89B AG settlement charge, and family DAP up 3% to 3.64B for September (Meta)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Meta reported Q3 revenue of $64.6 billion, up 26% year-over-year, while daily active people across its app family reached 3.64 billion. However, operating income plunged 37% to $12.99 billion, heavily dragged down by an extraordinary $9.89 billion state attorney general settlement charge. The results show durable core advertising momentum masked by hefty, one-time regulatory clearing costs.
