Anthropic Is Said To Guide IPO Price To Top Of Range Or Above

✍️ By Bloomberg

AI lab Anthropic is guiding its IPO pricing to the top of its range or higher, fueled by significant institutional oversubscription. This pricing action establishes the first public market benchmark for pure-play AI labs, effectively resetting valuation models for the private AI sector.

Anthropic PBC is guiding prospective investors in its initial public offering it expects to price the shares at the high end of the price range or above, according to people familiar with the matter.

The Claude chatbot maker and its seven co-founders are marketing 825 million shares in aggregate for $70 to $78 each, according to its filings with the US Securities and Exchange Commission. At that price, and including a $26 billion private placement, the IPO would raise $90.4 billion.

Banks are expected to stop taking orders at 4 p.m. on Tuesday in New York, the people said, asking not to be identified as the information isn’t public. At the top of the price range, Anthropic would have a fully diluted valuation of about $1.4 trillion.

Deliberations are ongoing and details of the offering could still change, the people said. A representative for Anthropic couldn’t immediately comment.

The pricing would top the $75 billion base offering from SpaceX, making it the biggest listing of all time. Still, Anthropic’s potential valuation could be smaller than Elon Musk’s company was when it made its debut. SpaceX had a $1.77 trillion market value when it priced its first-time share sale.

Anthropic’s IPO has drawn orders for close to seven times the shares available, Bloomberg News reported Sunday. Bankers have told prospective investors that existing backers will be prioritized by the company, and some would-be buyers will receive smaller allocations than they expect, people familiar with the matter have said.

Anthropic has been bolstering investor interest in the IPO with investor events including a livestreamed discussion between its Chief Executive Officer Dario Amodei and JPMorgan Chase & Co. CEO Jamie Dimon on Friday.

“I was impressed,” Bill Ackman said about Amodei in an interview after the event, which the billionaire fund manager attended in person. “He seemed like a very thoughtful, measured CEO.”

The company’s sales are growing rapidly, and so are its spending plans. Anthropic reported a revenue run rate of $81 billion as of Oct. 31, up $10 billion from the end of September, the filing shows. The firm behind Claude has commitments of about $71 billion due in the year through Sept. 30, 2027. Most are for computing power and chips.

The IPO is set to price on Wednesday, Nov. 18 and trade the following day, according to terms of the deal reviewed by Bloomberg.

Goldman Sachs Group Inc. and Morgan Stanley are leading Anthropic’s IPO, with participation from 20 other banks, the filing shows.
