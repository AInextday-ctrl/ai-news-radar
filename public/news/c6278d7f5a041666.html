SpaceX revealed in its Q3 2026 financial report that quarterly revenue surged 161% year-over-year to $14.58 billion, easily beating Wall Street estimates of $12.72 billion. Its AI division revenue soared 830% to $7.56 billion with an operating profit of $431 million, turning around from a $1.7 billion loss, driven by aggressive expansion in AI compute and Starlink satellite connectivity.
