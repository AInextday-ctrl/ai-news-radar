OpenAI Engineer Accused of Spying on Executives Ordered to Remain in Custody

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
A federal magistrate judge in San Francisco ordered former OpenAI engineer Abdulrahman Alfozan to remain in custody on corporate espionage charges, citing immense flight risks. Prosecutors revealed that the Saudi Arabian government offered unconditional financial backing to fund his bail, which the judge deemed equivalent to a blank check. The ruling stems from allegations that Alfozan illicitly accessed and stole confidential internal data from OpenAI executives.
