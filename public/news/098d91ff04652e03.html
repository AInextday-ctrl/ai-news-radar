Europe's three biggest industrial companies are cashing in on the AI boom, as Schneider and Siemens expand further into software, while ABB focuses on equipment (Aaron Kirchfeld/Financial Times)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Europe's top three industrial giants—Schneider Electric, Siemens, and ABB—are capitalizing on the global AI infrastructure surge with diverging playbooks. While Schneider and Siemens push aggressively into industrial software and energy automation, ABB doubles down on high-demand electrical equipment for data centers. The unprecedented power and compute demands of generative AI have turned these legacy titans into the primary beneficiaries of the ongoing tech boom.
