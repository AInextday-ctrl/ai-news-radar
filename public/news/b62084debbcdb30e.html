Alibaba, Tencent, and Huawei are pouring billions into data centers in Shaoguan, a hinterland region with cheap power and a cool climate, angering some locals (Financial Times)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Chinese technology giants Alibaba, Tencent, and Huawei are committing tens of billions of yuan to construct massive AI-focused data centers in Shaoguan, Guangdong province. The hinterland region was designated as a core hub in the national computing framework due to its low-cost hydropower resources and cooler climate. However, the surging electricity demands, land allocations, and environmental impacts associated with these high-density facilities have triggered local friction regarding ecological burdens and economic equity.
