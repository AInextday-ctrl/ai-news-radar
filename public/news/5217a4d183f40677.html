📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

FinTech Global Full Report

Databento, a platform supplying market data to the modern financial industry, has secured $100m in a Series C round that values the business at $1.1bn.

Fifth Down Capital, which backed Databento in its early days, led the investment. Almost every one of the firm’s existing backers returned to take part, among them NEA, DRW Venture Capital, Tribe Capital and Hedosophia.

Fitch Ventures also came on board as a strategic investor, adding the viewpoint of a major financial information business with significant experience in credit ratings, financial data and market analysis.

The raise comes only months after Databento closed a $97m Series B earlier in the year. In the period since that deal, the company has grown its revenue more than twofold and has stayed profitable throughout.

Databento’s client base spans top quantitative trading houses, brokerages, asset managers, commodities businesses and digital asset firms. It has also built a notable foothold in the artificial intelligence space, securing enterprise agreements with five of the six biggest frontier AI labs.

At the time the Series C closed, the company employed 39 people, meaning it crossed the $1.1bn valuation mark with a much smaller workforce than conventional market data vendors typically need.

Databento will put the fresh capital towards further development of its market data infrastructure and towards broadening its coverage into more asset classes.

The firm delivers both live and historical data covering futures, options, equities and indices, all accessible via a single API. Because it controls every layer of its technology, from the original data source through to final delivery, Databento says it is the first offering of its type to pair the speed and dependability required by the world’s top trading firms with a far easier experience for developers.

Databento co-founder and CEO Christina Qi said, “In this AI era, we’re seeing remarkably small teams build billion-dollar companies, but that level of efficiency is unprecedented in the market data industry.
