Vertical Slice Full Report

The move comes days after the highly anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto VI.

Chris Kerr Nov 26, 2026 November 26 2026 2:47 PM 2 min read Share Share Share Share Share Email Copy UK workers at Grand Theft Auto maker Rockstar Games have voted against a union recognition deal that would have seen the Rockstar Game Workers Union (RGWU) become the largest recognised union in the UK video game sector.

Rockstar confirmed the news in a statement to press. Vertical Slice understands 1,544 employees—93.2 percent of eligible workers—participated in the ballot.

"We are extraordinarily proud of our team, the culture we've built together and the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI. This result reflects our team's strong support for continuing the direct relationships, collaboration and open dialogue that have helped make Rockstar Games so innovative and strong. As we look ahead, we're excited to build on all we've accomplished together," said a company rep.

RGWU is part of the Independent Workers Union of Great Britain (IWGB).

In the UK, union recognition means an employer has officially agreed to deal with a union representing a section of workers. Although it clears the way for formal negotiations, workers in the country do not need their employer's permission to join a trade union. That's why RGWU already exists.

The vote was open to 1,600 employees below the level of director and ran from November 12 to November 26, overlapping with the long-awaited launch of Grand Theft Auto VI.

The IWGB said 25 percent of eligible workers voted in favour of the deal, with 75 percent voting against. It added the result "does not prevent Rockstar workers from organising with the IWGB or exercising their trade union rights, but means management will not be formally committed to regular meetings with union representatives."

"For anyone at Rockstar who believes in a fairer workplace, a fairer sector and a fairer society—this isn’t a sign to retreat, it’s a sign to double down on what we stand for. Hundreds of our coworkers voted in favour of our union at Rockstar, yet sadly we recognise we have more work to do to build understanding and win the trust of more of our colleagues. We’re ready to put in the effort to make that happen," added the IWGB Rockstar Game Workers Union.

Workers have chosen to shoot down a deal that included pay negotiation frameworks covering overtime and bonuses, leave and pensions, and more. It would have also provided consultation rights over HR policies and the use of generative AI in the workplace.

The news comes with Rockstar and a group of fired workers awaiting the outcome of a high-profile employment tribunal centred on allegations of union busting. Last year, Rockstar dismissed over 30 workers who claimed they were ousted for engaging in trade union activity. The studio says they were let go for leaking confidential information in a union Discord server.
