Anthropic Founders to Cash Out $1.75 Billion in Blockbuster IPO

✍️ By Bloomberg News

Anthropic revealed in an updated S-1 filing that its seven co-founders plan to sell $1.75 billion worth of stock during next week's IPO. This move allows the founders to cash out, with each taking home approximately $250 million at the offering's midpoint.

  • [Core Event] The seven co-founders of Anthropic (Ahead of the upcoming IPO): Plan to collectively sell $1.75 billion in personal stock
  • [Root Cause & Mechanism] Capitalizing on the AI hype cycle for personal liquidity
  • [Key Metrics] Total value $1.75B, approx $250M per founder.

📰 Full In-Depth Coverage

Bloomberg Full Report

Each co-founder is offering shares worth about $250 million at the midpoint of the IPO range. At that valuation, each will have a net worth of about $18 billion, according to the Bloomberg Billionaires Index.

San Francisco-based Anthropic is seeking to raise as much as $90.4 billion at a valuation of about $1.4 trillion in its initial public offering and a private placement, according to the filing. The IPO is set to price Nov. 18 and begin trading the next day, according to terms of the deal reviewed by Bloomberg.

Founders selling stock in their company during its public offering — especially in such large quantities — can send a bearish signal to the market. Still, the relative scale of Anthropic's IPO and changing attitudes toward such insider sales mean they're not likely to spook potential buyers, according to Jay R. Ritter, an emeritus professor at the University of Florida's Warrington College of Business.

"It's definitely on the high side in terms of dollar amount," said Ritter, whose research focuses on public offerings, but "the market has gotten more comfortable with founders and large shareholders taking some money off the table."

He pointed to Google's 2004 IPO, when Larry Page and Sergey Brin each sold about $41 million of stock and then-CEO Eric Schmidt sold about $31 million.

"There are definitely precedents," he added.

The Anthropic sales, while large in dollar terms, represent only about 1.5% of each co-founder's total stake in the artificial intelligence firm. And it's not the first time that company insiders have taken money off the table: Co-founders Dario Amodei and his sister Daniela both participated in tender offers in June 2025 and April 2026, according to the prospectus.

Other co-founders include Tom Brown, Jack Clark, Jared Kaplan, Sam McCandlish and Christopher Olah.

The sales give the executives added liquidity. The Amodeis last year received relatively small cash salaries of about $650,000 apiece, while the vast majority of their fortunes remain tied up in Anthropic stock.

"Elon Musk isn't selling any of his SpaceX shares, but he doesn't have to worry about where his next meal is coming from," Ritter said. "You've got some people with enormous paper wealth all in one stock, and not a whole lot of cash. And so selling a little bit is something that the market doesn't get enormously concerned about."

🤖 AI Radar Technical Deep-Dive

It’s not so much about changing the world with AI, as it is about changing the founders' bank balances. Cashing out $250 million each before the paint is even dry on the IPO is a classic move of leaving the 'AI revolution' for the retail investors while taking the cash to the beach.
