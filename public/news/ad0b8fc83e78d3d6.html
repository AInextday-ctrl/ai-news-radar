GTCR and Elliott agree to acquire and take private CCC, a Nasdaq-listed car insurance software provider, for $4.6B; the deal values CCC at $6.1B including debt (Bloomberg)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Private equity firm GTCR and activist investor Elliott Investment Management have agreed to acquire and take private Nasdaq-listed automotive insurance software provider CCC in a $4.6 billion cash deal, valuing the company at $6.1 billion including debt. The acquisition aims to capitalize on CCC's highly recurring cash flow and deep defensive moat across collision repair and claims software, away from public market scrutiny.
