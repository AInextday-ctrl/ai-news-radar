Google says Cloud in its London region is "experiencing issues" starting on Monday at 23:08 PDT; a source says a major Revolut outage is due to Google Cloud (Bloomberg)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Google Cloud's London infrastructure region suffered a widespread service disruption beginning late Monday, triggering outages across multiple enterprise clients in the UK. The incident notably incapacitated core banking and transaction processing systems for fintech unicorn Revolut, leaving millions of mobile banking customers unable to access accounts or complete transfers. The severe breakdown underscores the architectural vulnerability of modern fintech platforms relying on single-provider cloud infrastructures without resilient multi-region failovers.
