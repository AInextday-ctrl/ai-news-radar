Note-taking tool Otter.ai raised $320M in non-dilutive capital from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund; PitchBook says it raised $50M prior to this round (Ivan Mehta/TechCrunch)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
AI meeting collaboration platform Otter.ai has secured $320 million in non-dilutive capital from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund. The financing vehicle does not dilute equity and generates returns anchored to the commercial yields of marketing spend. Having recently crossed the $100 million ARR milestone, Otter plans to deploy the capital into enterprise expansion, go-to-market acceleration, and product innovation without surrendering operational control to venture backers.
