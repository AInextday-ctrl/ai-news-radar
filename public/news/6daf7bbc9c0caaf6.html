Robotic therapy for stroke patients

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Researchers from Purdue University and MIT have engineered an AI-powered rehabilitation robot to address the severe shortage of physical therapists for five million stroke survivors annually. Utilizing generative AI and real-time force feedback learned from human therapists, the system dynamically delivers optimal mechanical assistance for motor recovery exercises.
