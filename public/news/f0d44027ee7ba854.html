The Verge AI Full Report

I suppose I must issue a hearty congratulations to Jensen Huang. Huang is CEO of Nvidia again, to the surprise of basically everyone except President Donald Trump and Vice President JD Vance, who made sure it happened.

Now, those of us who had some kind of basic education on what a “free market” is might find it objectionable to have the American president strong-arming a major American company. But our billionaire tech overlords care about the “free market” exactly as much as they care about “free speech,” which is to say not at all. Watching Silicon Valley cheer for what is, effectively, a sign of a command economy has certainly been bracing. But then, why would they give a shit? Their only real beliefs are that they should remain rich and above the law. Jensen may not have exchanged emails with Jeffrey Epstein, but he’s squarely at the Epstein table now.

"The deal was reportedly brokered by Peter Thiel’s very own boy"

I have argued here, in the past, that the Epstein case laid bare the real politics of the revanchist American right wing: total elite impunity. Well, here it is again! If you are close enough with Trump, it doesn’t matter if you may have jeopardized national security by fucking an alleged Chinese spy. You won’t even be investigated for it before the American president calls in to your board of directors’ meeting to demand you be reinstated. I mean, it is the era of gangster tech regulation — what were any of us expecting? The deal was reportedly brokered by Peter Thiel’s very own boy, Vance.

Just so we can be absolutely clear about what transpired at Nvidia, let’s quickly go through the facts as we know them. A Chinese citizen named Alice Huang acquired the company she renamed Megaspeed, the Singaporean data firm at the center of this inquiry, in 2023. At some point, Huang met Huang, and the two were seen together at a dinner that December. From 2024 to 2025, Megaspeed received more than 200 Nvidia shipments.

In September 2025, the US government began investigating Megaspeed for possible violations of chip export controls; that October, the State Department told Trump of Jensen Huang’s affair with Alice Huang, who was described as a Chinese intelligence asset. Also in October, The New York Times first reported on Megaspeed’s relationship with Nvidia. In December, Trump — who has a close relationship with Jensen Huang — allowed Nvidia to sell more powerful chips to China. In March 2026, Marco Rubio warned the government about Alice, and Trump nonetheless invited Huang to a visit in China. In October, The New York Times reported on the affair. Shortly after Huang sent a note to Nvidia admitting to it, several other reports of infidelity — this time with Nvidia employees — surfaced. On November 12th, Huang resigned. On November 17th, during the Nvidia earnings call, the board’s chair said that the company “does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.” On November 23rd, Huang was back.

"This level of open corruption is genuinely impressive"

What changed between last week and this week? A week was not enough time to truly review all the information on Huang and his paramour, Alice “No Relation” Huang, the international woman of mystery. The explanation seems to be that Nvidia’s board succumbed to a pressure campaign from Huang’s fellow billionaires, and a direct intervention from the president and vice president of the United States of America.

Sure, all these CEOs ostensibly wanted Huang back at Nvidia to keep the AI trade going. Still, watching the likes of Epic CEO Tim Sweeney, former Uber CEO Travis Kalanick, investing loudmouth Bill Ackman, and Elon Musk — who actually did appear in the Epstein files, and who seemed to be pretty friendly with Epstein! — pitch a fit last week was instructive. These men worried that they might be punished for wrongdoing, that they did not get big enough to be above the law. They have now been reassured, and I suppose that is what really matters. After all, Trump said on CNBC that he gave Nvidia’s board assurances that Jensen is “absolutely fine.”

This level of open corruption is genuinely impressive, and not just because Huang is CEO of Nvidia again. Almost in passing, Trump noted that he had “protected” Musk in his first term from “a certain incident [that] took place, a very tough incident.” In his own inimitable style, Trump told Jim Cramer, “You wouldn’t have Elon if it wasn’t for me.” Surely there is no possible downside when the CEOs of two of the biggest corporations on Earth owe their jobs to Trump personally.

"Surely there is no possible downside when the CEOs of two of the biggest corporations on Earth owe their jobs to Trump personally"

One thing that has perhaps emboldened this crew is the stance from professional politics-knowers — and House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries — that holding members of the Trump administration accountable might be a hindrance to governing. That accountability was part of the reason for the blue wave in November seems to have bypassed these pundits (and the presumptive House majority leader). But then, why should we believe that Democrats will thoroughly investigate this? Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer is claiming to want an investigation, but I am skeptical of how deep that investigation will go as long as Schumer leads it, and only partially because he’s been allied with Silicon Valley for more than a decade. He also has a daughter who works at OpenAI, a company that Nvidia invests in and that is dependent on Nvidia chips. How hard is he really willing to go?

Of course, there may be more to this than just pure economic self-interest. In celebrating Huang’s return, Trump posted a remarkable image. It is an altered picture of Trump himself, taken after a failed assassination attempt in 2024. Trump’s head has been replaced with Jensen Huang’s head. The American flag has been replaced with the Nvidia logo. Huang is waving a bright red bra. It seems I owe Sigmund Freud an apology. In the end, these men fear nothing more than losing power for sexual shenanigans — and especially losing power to a woman.

Leave aside the national security issues for a minute and focus on the two Nvidia employees. Dating subordinates is a serious conflict of interest; the CEO of Nestle lost his job for just that last year. This is a major violation for CEOs of normal companies, but it is common practice among the sleazeballs of Silicon Valley: Bill Gates is famous for it and it was plainly rampant at Google, a tone set by Sergey Brin himself. I am sure that you will be shocked, shocked to discover that in the wake of MeToo, Brin was among the men who suddenly swung right. I mean, Musk himself announced he was a Republican the day before Business Insider reported that he exposed himself to a flight attendant and paid hush money to cover it up.

"If you’re rich enough, and connected enough, everything is permitted"

The Republican Party has made the abuse of women without consequences one of its main issues; that the lesser violation of sleeping with subordinates would also be something the revanchists would defend seems obvious. That was clear from the botched investigation of the Epstein affair itself, the botched release of the Epstein files, and the assorted confirmation hearings of Pete Hegseth, Brett Kavanaugh, and Todd Blanche. (To say nothing of the Andrew Tate business.) But in all this, let’s not forget that Trump has been accused of sexual misconduct by no fewer than 28 women. So I’m sure it was an affront to all these guys that not only did Huang’s misconduct include a series of affairs, but he was also replaced by Colette Kress, who is known to be a woman.

I suppose we might as well pass by the various reports that Huang allegedly terrorized his employees. In light of everything else, this seems minor by comparison.

Anyway I had that all in mind when I saw Epic’s Sweeney celebrating the state intrusion into theoretically private companies by posting “Back in Black,” an oblique reference to Huang’s leather jackets that is also from the later, lesser period of AC/DC, after Bon Scott died and AC/DC became an AC/DC tribute band. He also called The New York Times’ reporting into Huang “lies.” Musk continued his streak of Sephiroth posting: “The mistake has been corrected.” Jason Calacanis, the All-In podcast’s village idiot, posted, “GPU Jesus has risen!” along with an openly blasphemous, AI-generated image of Huang as Christ.

Best of luck to Joon Kim, the Wachtell Lipton partner and former federal prosecutor who is conducting “a thorough and independent investigation” into Huang’s alleged misconduct on behalf of Nvidia’s board. I assume Kim has, like the rest of us, gotten the message loud and clear: If you’re rich enough, and connected enough, everything is permitted. Or perhaps, more succinctly: lol nothing matters.
