Foxconn agrees to acquire a 50% stake in Mitsubishi's automotive components unit for ~$727M; Mitsubishi will provide autonomous tech for Foxconn's EV platforms (Reed Stevenson/Bloomberg)

📖 Read Original Source Report ↗
Hon Hai Precision Industry (Foxconn) agreed to acquire a 50% stake in Mitsubishi Motors' automotive components unit for roughly 115 billion yen ($727 million). In return, Mitsubishi will license autonomous driving technology to power Foxconn's proprietary EV manufacturing platforms. The strategic buyout bolsters Foxconn's tier-one automotive credentials while bridging its critical technical gap in advanced autonomous driving integration.
