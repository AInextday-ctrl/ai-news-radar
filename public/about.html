About AI News Radar

An independent engineering think tank and technical publication delivering reproducible benchmarks, systems teardowns, and frontier AI research.

🎯 Our Mission: Engineering Truth Over Hype

Founded by a team of veteran systems architects, machine learning infrastructure engineers, and full-stack developers, AI News Radar (ainewsradar.xyz) was created with a single objective: to cut through synthetic marketing claims and deliver battle-tested, reproducible, and mathematically grounded AI engineering knowledge.

In an era where millions of AI summaries regurgitate press releases, real engineering value requires empirical verification. AI News Radar bridges the gap between theoretical research papers and production deployments—running code on physical silicon, measuring real memory footprints, profiling latency distributions, and evaluating agentic autonomy under adversarial conditions.

🧪 The Radar Systems & Hardware Lab

To maintain zero reliance on vendor marketing decks, our research team maintains a dedicated, on-premises benchmarking cluster:

  • High-Performance Compute Workstations: Equipped with dual NVIDIA RTX 4090 (24GB VRAM) and RTX 3090 (24GB VRAM) testbeds, utilized for measuring decode throughput, time-to-first-token (TTFT), and token generation costs across Ollama, vLLM, and SGLang runtimes.
  • Edge & Mobile Silicon Workstations: Apple Silicon M3 Max (64GB Unified Memory) and Intel Core Ultra edge systems, specifically configured for benchmarking Small Language Models (SLMs) such as Llama 3.2, Phi-4, and Gemma 2 under 4-bit/8-bit quantizations.
  • Global API Network Probes: Continuous synthetic transaction probes deployed across US East, US West, Europe Central, and Asia Pacific to track real-world latency, context caching cost reductions, and API stability under production loads.

📋 Code Reproducibility & Fact-Checking Pledge

Every flagship article adheres to our strict publishing manifesto:

  • 100% Executable Code: All Dockerfiles, bash commands, and Python implementation scripts are executed and verified inside fresh containers before publication. Exact package dependencies and CUDA runtime versions are explicitly specified.
  • Transparent Attribution: Direct links to primary arXiv preprints, GitHub commit hashes, and model weights on Hugging Face are provided for every claim.
  • Zero Commercial Sponsorship Bias: We do not accept sponsored reviews or promotional content from AI vendors. All tested software and hardware are acquired independently.
