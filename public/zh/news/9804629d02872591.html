AI金融代理平台Model ML获投

✍️ 记者 / 供稿: Bloomberg News

专注于构建金融AI智能体的初创公司Model ML完成新一轮融资，公司估值达到15亿美元

⚡ 核心要点提炼与关键事实

  • 【核心事件】Model ML（AI金融初创公司）在金融科技风投市场：以15亿美元估值完成新一轮融资
  • 【起因与机理】利用AI代理解决金融领域高频重复性工作的效率瓶颈
  • 【关键数据与实测】完成新一轮融资，估值达15亿美元。
  • 【各方立场与态度】旨在自动化银行及投资机构的重复性任务。

📰 深度报道原文实录

Bloomberg 权威实录

Model ML landed $250 million in a Series B round at a $1.5 billion valuation.

It designs AI agents that handle routine tasks for bankers, consultants, and investors.

The new funding will go into research, engineering, and larger customer deployments.

Go to any junior banker and ask him or her where the week went, and you’ll hear the same answer. Pitch decks and diligence memos. Time spent in font work and double-checking numbers. It’s clever people doing things that should be done by a machine.

Model ML raises $250 million at a $1.5 billion valuation to solve this problem. The startup develops AI agents for bankers, consultants, and investors, reducing the time spent on spreadsheets, slide decks, and late-night email threads.

General Atlantic and QED Investors co-led the round, the company said in a statement on Tuesday. Other investors included Evolution Equity Partners, Airtree, Forestay Capital, Y Combinator, Latitude, Inception Fund, and 13Books Capital.

Model ML’s Series B at $250M is more than 3x larger than its $75M Series A just a year ago. Nigel Morris of QED summed it up in a single statement, noting that he hadn’t seen a team grow quicker. To say that at such an early age in the company’s history is quite a statement.

Model ML works like a virtual partner for all bankers. The firm designs personal AI agents that get to know its users’ clients, deals, processes, and output. The more you use them, the better they will get.

They are also at work behind the scenes. The agents operate within the system already existing in the business and monitor for pertinent news. They then decide what is important and draft completed reports, such as an analysis of a company that a banker writes about.

The company claims it can reduce a task from days to minutes. Banks are extremely conscious about security and they are correct. It can be deployed on any infrastructure, keeping the data on the same servers the companies use.

Model ML states that customer information is not used for AI model training. Chaz and Arnie Englander are the brothers behind this startup. Chaz is the CEO. The two founded a small family office after selling previous startups, they said.

Pitches were still coming in, but they wanted to do a better job of filtering out founders and reducing each business to a one-pager, so they created software to do just that. So they decided that they were better builders than investors.

In the third quarter, the company reported annual recurring revenue had increased by 12x and daily active users increased by 26x from last year. These are company numbers, not audited. Nonetheless, they suggest actual demand.

The massive demand suggests that investors are hoping that AI agents will be able to perform tedious and mundane tasks, rather than just answering questions. Banking is a natural test! The work is repetitive, voluminous, and costly.

The new cash will support London and New York research and engineering teams, more product development and bigger customer deployments. This year Model ML has also set up offices in Paris, Madrid, Tokyo, Singapore and Dubai.

If you’re in the finance, consulting, or investing business, Model ML wants to take away the mundane stuff first. Think about what you would delegate if you had to do so tomorrow. Then see if large companies employ agents for the second stage of the work.
