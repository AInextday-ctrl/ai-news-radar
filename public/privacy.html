Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 · Governing ainewsradar.xyz

At AI News Radar (accessible at ainewsradar.xyz), we are deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy and digital rights of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is utilized, and your rights under global privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. In general, you can navigate our research and radar intelligence without revealing personal information:

  • Server Log Files: Like virtually all standard web servers, we automatically record anonymous request logs, including your IP address, browser user-agent, referring/exit pages, timestamp, and requested URL paths. These logs are used strictly for network diagnostic security, rate-limiting malicious bots, and monitoring server health.
  • Direct Communications: If you contact us via email (contact@ainewsradar.xyz) or through our contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message contents you provide to respond to your inquiry.

2. Cookies, Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We partner with third-party vendors, most notably Google AdSense, to serve contextually relevant advertisements when you browse our site:

  • DoubleClick DART Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on ainewsradar.xyz based on prior visits to our site or other websites across the Internet.
  • Opt-Out Mechanisms: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookie usage for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to:

  • Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected;
  • Request deletion of any personal data collected;
  • Direct a business that sells personal data not to sell the consumer's personal data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. GDPR Data Protection Rights

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), every user is entitled to:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request erasure of your data under certain conditions.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our data compliance desk at privacy@ainewsradar.xyz.
