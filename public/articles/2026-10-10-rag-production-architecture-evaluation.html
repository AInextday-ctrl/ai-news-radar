Enterprise RAG Architecture & Retrieval Optimization: Hybrid Search, ColBERT Reranking & Multimodal Parsing

✦ Executive Research Brief · Hardware-Verified in Radar Lab ✦

A comprehensive production blueprint for enterprise Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Explores hybrid dense-sparse fusion (BM25 + BGE-M3), ColBERT late interaction reranking, and semantic chunking on unstructured PDF documents.

Simple naive RAG architectures (vector search + Top-K chunk concatenation) consistently fail in enterprise environments when confronted with complex domain terminologies, structured tables, and cross-document reasoning. In this field guide, we outline the production-proven Hybrid Search & Reranking Architecture engineered at our laboratory.

01 The Retrieval Funnel: From Recall to Precision

High-accuracy retrieval requires decoupling the candidate recall stage from the precision scoring stage:

  • Stage 1 (Hybrid Recall): Execute parallel BM25 sparse keyword search and dense bi-encoder vector search (e.g., BAAI/bge-m3), retrieving the top 100 candidate chunks;
  • Stage 2 (Reciprocal Rank Fusion - RRF): Merge candidate rank lists using rank-based weighting (k=60), ensuring high tolerance against out-of-vocabulary acronyms;
  • Stage 3 (Cross-Encoder / ColBERT Rerank): Score the top 30 chunks through a multi-vector late-interaction reranker (bge-reranker-large or ColBERT-v2), truncating to the top 5 highest-relevance contexts.
