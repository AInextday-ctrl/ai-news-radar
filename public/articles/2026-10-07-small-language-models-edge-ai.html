On-Device Small Language Models (SLMs) Deployment: Llama 3.2, Phi-4 & Gemma 2 Quantization & Fine-Tuning Guide

✦ Executive Research Brief · Hardware-Verified in Radar Lab ✦

A practical engineering guide for deploying sub-10B parameter models on Apple Silicon and edge hardware. We analyze GGUF bit precision (Q4_K_M vs Q8_0), Ollama integration, memory footprints, and low-latency on-device agent inference.

Cloud-hosted frontier models carry non-trivial privacy, latency, and subscription liabilities. With the release of Llama 3.2 (1B/3B), Microsoft's Phi-4 (14B), and Google's Gemma 2 (2B/9B), running high-capability reasoning models entirely on local developer workstations and edge devices is now an enterprise reality.

01 Precision vs Accuracy: The Quantization Frontier

Quantizing FP16 weights down to 4-bit and 8-bit integers is essential to fit models into workstation Unified Memory:

  • Q4_K_M: Provides the ideal balance between memory footprint (approx. 0.65x model size in GB) and perplexity retention (>98% of FP16 benchmark scores);
  • AWQ (Activation-aware Weight Quantization): Protects the critical 1% salient weights, delivering near-lossless reasoning for mathematical tasks.
